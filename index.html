Private property investment & letting

A residential property portfolio held and let across South London.

Jaman Properties Ltd is a privately held UK limited company that owns and lets residential property. We manage our portfolio directly, holding each property for the long term rather than trading it on.

What we do

We acquire, hold and let residential property in and around London, taking a long-term view on each asset rather than a short-term one. Day-to-day management, compliance and tenant relationships are handled directly, not outsourced wholesale.

  • Long-term residential letting, not short-let or trading
  • Direct portfolio management and compliance
  • A small, owner-managed operation, not a fund
