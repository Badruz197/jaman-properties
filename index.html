About

A privately held property company

Jaman Properties Ltd is a private limited company registered in England and Wales that owns and lets residential property.

The company acquires and holds residential property, letting it on long-term tenancies. It is owner-managed and operates on its own account — it is not a fund, an agency, or an adviser to third parties.

Approach

Held, not traded

Property is bought to be kept. The company takes a long-term view on each asset rather than a short-term one, and handles letting, compliance and day-to-day management directly.

  • Long-term residential letting
  • Direct management and compliance
  • A small, owner-managed operation
