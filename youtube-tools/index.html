YouTube Library Tools

An internal tool used by Jaman Properties Ltd.

YouTube Library Tools is a private, internal application. Its only user is the company's director, who uses it to organise their own YouTube account. It is not offered to the public and does not accept sign-ups.

What it does

  • Lists the playlists in the signed-in YouTube account.
  • Creates new private playlists and adds chosen videos to them.
  • Renames existing playlists.
  • Reads public video information (titles, channels, lengths) to help decide what to keep.

It never deletes videos or playlists and never posts, comments, uploads or changes anything else in the account.
