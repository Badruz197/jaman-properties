Privacy policy

GWS CLI · Last updated 24 October 2026

This policy covers GWS CLI (the "application"), a command-line tool used by Badruz Jaman to work with his own Google accounts from his own computer. The application is used only by its operator, on his own Google accounts.

What the application accesses

With the account holder's permission, given through Google sign-in, the application calls Google APIs for the services it has been set up for: Gmail, Google Calendar, Google Drive, Google Sheets and Google Docs. The permissions are those shown on Google's consent screen at sign-in.

The application acts only on commands typed by the account holder, such as searching or reading mail, calendar events, files and spreadsheets.

How data is used and stored

  • Data returned by Google is used only to carry out the commands the account holder types.
  • OAuth credentials are stored on the account holder's own computer, in per-account configuration folders, and are not copied to any server run by the application's author.
  • When the account holder uses an AI assistant (Claude, made by Anthropic) to work on the output of a command, that output may be read by the assistant. No other third party receives it.
  • No data is sold, shared with third parties or used for advertising.

Google API Services

The application's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. See also the Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access

Access can be withdrawn at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
